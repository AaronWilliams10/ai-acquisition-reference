# ICP

Fictional ICP for this reference implementation. The seller is a fictional studio that builds and runs AI-assisted acquisition systems for B2B companies on a monthly retainer.

## One line

Founder-led B2B service or software companies that already sell successfully, where the founder is visible online, and where new pipeline depends too much on referrals, events or the founder personally.

## Must have

- **B2B.** Sells to businesses.
- **A clear offer.** You can describe what they sell in one line from their website.
- **Proof they already sell.** Customers, case studies, revenue mentions, or hiring.
- **A reachable decision-maker.** Founder, CEO, owner, or a head of function who says they hold budget.
- **Can afford the retainer** ($5k–$8k/month, example pricing only). In practice: high-ticket or recurring contracts and roughly $500k+ annual revenue.

## Strong fit

- Pipeline depends on referrals, conferences or the founder's network.
- About to hire, or just hired, a first SDR or AE.
- Posts on X at least weekly about their business, sales or growth.
- US, UK, Canada, Australia, New Zealand or Ireland.
- 5–50 people.

## Not a fit

- B2C, local retail, hospitality.
- Students, side projects, pre-revenue.
- Low-ticket self-serve products. Outbound costs more than the customer is worth.
- 50+ staff with a sales team and procurement. Not rejected, but a different sale: MEDIUM at best.

## What they say out loud

lead-scout listens for these phrases. They map to the listening queries in `src/run.ts`.

| Phrase | What it usually means |
|---|---|
| "founder-led sales", "I still do all the sales" | Founder is the bottleneck |
| "all referrals", "word of mouth", "feast or famine" | No predictable channel |
| "every client came from a conference" | Pipeline only exists around events |
| "hiring our first SDR" | Budget exists, outbound is now a priority |
| "recommendations for an outbound agency" | Actively choosing a vendor this month |
